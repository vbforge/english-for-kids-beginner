Follow the order

Each track recycles the previous ones. Skipping ahead breaks the spiral.

⏱️

1–2 weeks per track

6–12 sessions of 15–20 minutes. Stretch to 3 weeks if the child needs more practice.

✅

Check before moving on

Only move to the next track when the child uses the current structures confidently — in real speech, not just exercises.

📖

Every track has 2 pages

Lesson (grammar) + Tasks (reading, listening, speaking, writing). Both are linked on every track.

👨‍👩‍👧 For parents & teachers — pacing and how to use this page

⚠️ Important about pacing

This roadmap is designed for roughly 15–18 months at a relaxed pace — but it flexes:

  • A single track can be stretched to 3–4 weeks if more practice is needed.
  • Take a "review month" between levels if the child feels wobbly.
  • Realistically: 1.5–2 years to finish comfortably — that's fine.
  • Rule #1: confidence in the current track > speed to the next one.

📚 How the two pages work together

📘 Lesson page (blue) — the grammar rule, examples, vocabulary table, and quick practice.

✍️ Task page (orange) — reading, listening, speaking prompts and a short writing task. This is what turns grammar into real language.

Do both in the same week. Grammar alone doesn't stick — it sticks when the child uses it in a story, a question, or a note.
