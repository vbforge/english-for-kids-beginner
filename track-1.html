To be — I am, You are

Meet the most useful verb in English. By the end of this track, your child can introduce themselves, say how they feel, and ask the same questions back.

1 Goal of this track

By the end, the child can confidently say and ask:

  • I'm + name / age / feeling / role
  • You're + description
  • Am I…? / Are you…? questions
  • Short answers: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.

2 The rule

In English, the verb to be changes depending on who is doing the action. For I and you, the forms are simple and short.

Positive

I am … Я є… / Мене звати… / Мені … років
You are … Ти є… / Ти …
Short forms: I am = I'm · You are = You're

Negative

I am not … Я не є…
You are not … Ти не є…
Short forms: I am not = I'm not · You are not = You aren't / You're not

Questions

Am I …? Чи я…?
Are you …? Чи ти…?
Short answers: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.  ·  Yes, you are. / No, you aren't.

3 Examples

About me (I)

  • I'm Max. — Мене звати Макс.
  • I'm ten. — Мені десять.
  • I'm happy. — Я щасливий.
  • I'm a pupil. — Я учень.
  • I'm not tired. — Я не втомлений.
  • Am I late? — Я запізнився?

About you (You)

  • You're my friend. — Ти мій друг.
  • You're funny. — Ти смішний.
  • You aren't sad. — Ти не сумний.
  • Are you OK? — Ти в порядку?

5 Quick practice

Exercise 1 · Fill in am or are

  1. I ten.
  2. You my friend.
  3. I happy.
  4. You not sad.
  5. I late?
  6. you OK?
Answers: 1. am · 2. are · 3. am · 4. are · 5. Am · 6. Are

Exercise 2 · Make the sentence negative

  1. I'm tired. →
  2. You're sad. →
  3. I'm late. →
Answers: 1. I'm not tired. · 2. You aren't sad. · 3. I'm not late.

Exercise 3 · Make questions

  1. I ? (late)
  2. you ? (OK)
  3. you ? (happy)
Answers: 1. Am I late? · 2. Are you OK? · 3. Are you happy?

✅ Ready for Track 2?

  • Say You're + a description
  • Build negatives: I'm not / You aren't
  • Ask: Am I…? / Are you…?
  • Answer short: Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
  • Hear the difference between am and are in speech
