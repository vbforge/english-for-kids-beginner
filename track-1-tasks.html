Task Pack · Track 01 Use after the lesson

Practice Pack — I am, You are

Grammar becomes real when the child uses it. These four short tasks take about 20–30 minutes total and turn "I am / You are" into language the child can actually speak and write.

📖 Reading Short text with "I am / You are" — 5 minutes.
🎧 Listening Parent reads aloud, child listens — 5 minutes.
🗣️ Speaking 3 real-life prompts — 10 minutes.
✍️ Writing A tiny "About me" note — 5 minutes.
📖
Task 1 · Reading 5 minutes

Read the text. Then answer the questions below.

Hi! This is me.

Hi! I'm Mia. I'm ten years old. I'm a pupil. I'm happy today. My friend is Tom. He's ten too. You're my friend, too — nice to meet you!

Привіт! Я Міа. Мені десять років. Я учениця. Я щаслива сьогодні. Мій друг — Том. Йому теж десять. Ти мій друг також — приємно познайомитись!
  1. What is the girl's name? Як звати дівчинку?
  2. How old is she? Скільки їй років?
  3. Is she a pupil? Вона учениця?
  4. How does she feel today? Як вона почувається сьогодні?
  5. Is Tom ten too? Тому теж десять?
Answer key
1. She's Mia.   2. She's ten.   3. Yes, she is.   4. She's happy.   5. Yes, he is.
🎧
Task 2 · Listening 5 minutes

Parent reads the dialogue aloud twice. Child listens and fills in the missing words.

Dialogue: At the door

Anna: Hi! Anna. you Ben?

Ben: Yes, Ben. Nice to meet you!

Anna: Nice to meet you too. you OK?

Ben: Yes, fine. And you?

Anna: happy today!

Show full script (for the parent)
Anna: Hi! I'm Anna. Are you Ben?
Ben: Yes, I'm Ben. Nice to meet you!
Anna: Nice to meet you too. Are you OK?
Ben: Yes, I'm fine. And you?
Anna: I'm happy today!
Answer key
1. I'm   2. Are   3. I'm   4. Are   5. I'm   6. I'm
🗣️
Task 3 · Speaking 10 minutes

Do these out loud together. The child answers in full sentences. No writing yet — just talking.

Prompt 1
Introduce yourself.
"I'm ___. I'm ___ years old. I'm a ___."
Prompt 2
How are you today?
"I'm happy / fine / tired / OK."
Prompt 3
Ask a friend.
"Are you OK? Are you happy? Are you ten?"

💡 Tip for the parent: Answer back in full sentences too. Model the language. If the child says "I'm fine" instead of "I'm fine, thank you", don't correct — just repeat it naturally and move on.

✍️
Task 4 · Writing 5 minutes

Write 3 short sentences about yourself. Use the word bank if you need help.

I'm happy fine tired OK ten a pupil years old

Example: I'm Alex. I'm ten years old. I'm happy.

📌 Note for the parent

  • Spread these tasks across the week — one per day works well.
  • Do not correct every mistake. Focus on one thing at a time (e.g., "I'm" vs "Are you").
  • Reading and Listening are receptive — the child can just listen and enjoy.
  • Speaking and Writing are productive — this is where the grammar actually sticks.
  • If the child struggles with Task 3 or 4 — go back to the lesson, review the rule, and try again the next day.
